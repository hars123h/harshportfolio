i. The doctor

Seventeen years, one way of working.

Dr. Kunal Bhatia is a prosthodontist: a specialist in rebuilding teeth, from a single crown to a full mouth. He still sees every patient himself.

Most of the people who come to me have already been told something frightening: that their teeth can’t be saved, or that they need work costing lakhs. My job is to look again, slowly, and tell them the truth, even when the truth is a smaller bill.

I trained as a prosthodontist because I like the hard cases: worn bites, failing bridges, smiles that need rebuilding from the ground up. Those cases need time, so I only see a few patients a day.

Every plan is drawn up with you, modelled in 3D, tried in your mouth as a temporary, and only then made permanent, by hand, in our own lab.

2009

Opens a two-chair practice

Fresh from an MDS in prosthodontics, with one assistant and a promise to explain every plan twice.

2013

An in-house lab

A ceramist joins, so crowns and veneers are made one floor down and shade-matched in daylight.

2017

Guided implant surgery

3D scanning and printed surgical guides become the standard for every implant.

2021

Teaching other dentists

Hands-on courses in full-mouth rehabilitation for dentists across North India.

2026

Still one patient at a time

6,000 smiles later, the appointments are still long and the plans still handwritten.
