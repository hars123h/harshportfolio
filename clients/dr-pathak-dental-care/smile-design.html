Treatments / Smile design

Small changes, a smile that’s more you.

Smile design starts with your face: how much tooth shows when you talk, the line of your lips, the shape of your teeth. The plan is then built from the simplest fix up: whitening, smoothing an uneven edge, bonding a gap, or veneers.

Is it for you?

You might need this if…

  • Teeth are stained or yellowed
  • A small gap, chip or uneven edge bothers you
  • Teeth look worn, short or mismatched
  • A wedding, interview or big photo is coming up

Try it

Design a smile, one step at a time.

Turn treatments on and off to see what each one changes. In the clinic we start with the simplest step that gets you the smile you want.

  • WhiteningLifts years of tea, coffee and food stains in about an hour, with your gums protected.
  • BondingTooth-coloured resin sculpted onto the teeth closes a small gap in a single visit.
  • ReshapingEnameloplasty gently smooths a chipped or uneven edge. Painless, done in minutes.
  • VeneersThin ceramic shells change colour, shape and size together, for a complete makeover.

What to expect

How it goes, visit by visit

  1. 1

    Smile study

    Photos and a look at your face, lips and bite. You tell us what you’d like to change.

  2. 2

    Preview

    Where possible, a mock-up lets you see the change on your own teeth before any work is done.

  3. 3

    Treatment

    Least to most: whitening, reshaping, bonding, then veneers only if they’re needed.

  4. 4

    Keep it

    Regular cleaning, and going easy on tea, coffee and tobacco, keeps the result bright.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Does whitening damage teeth?

Professional whitening done under supervision is safe for enamel. Some people get mild sensitivity for a day or two.

Will whitening change my crowns or fillings?

No, it only lightens natural teeth. If you have crowns on front teeth, we whiten first and match any new work to the new shade.

How long do veneers last?

Ceramic veneers commonly last 10 years or more with good care.

Are veneers reversible?

Usually a thin layer of enamel is reshaped, so treat veneers as a permanent choice. Bonding is the more reversible option.
