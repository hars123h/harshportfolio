Root canal, step by step

Scroll through a root canal, the way it’s done here.

Seven steps, all under the microscope. No guesswork, no rush.

01 / 07

  1. 01

    The problem

    Decay or injury lets bacteria reach the pulp: the nerves and blood vessels inside the tooth. That causes sensitivity, pain, swelling or a darkening tooth.

  2. 02

    Numb, gently

    The gum is numbed with a spray first, then the tooth. Once it is numb, you should feel no pain.

  3. 03

    Isolate with a rubber dam

    A soft rubber sheet seals the tooth off from the rest of your mouth: a bacteria-free field, and no chemicals or debris at the back of your throat.

  4. 04

    See it at 16×

    Every root canal here is done under an operating microscope, so even the tiniest canal is found and treated, and the most tooth structure is saved.

  5. 05

    Measure and clean

    An apex locator measures each canal’s exact working length. Rotary files shape the canals and ultrasonic irrigation flushes out bacteria and debris.

  6. 06

    Seal in 3D

    The canals are sealed with warm material that flows into every branch, including accessory canals, leaving no space for bacteria to return.

  7. 07

    Protect with a crown

    A cap restores the tooth’s strength so it lasts for years. In some cases a simple composite filling is enough.

Software-guided implants

Would you fly with a pilot who flies by hand?

An implant has to sit at an exact depth, angle and position to last. Here your jaw is scanned in 3D, the surgery is planned on software around your bone and nerves, and a custom acrylic guide steers the drill.

  • Usually no gum cutting or stitches, just a small opening
  • Less bleeding, faster healing
  • A crown aligned with your other teeth

Technology

The kit behind the precision.

All equipment

  • Operating microscope

    Up to 16× magnification for every root canal and filling. You can’t treat what you can’t see.

  • Zeiss loupes

    4× magnification when a patient can’t recline fully, for example with neck or back pain.

  • Apex locator

    Measures each canal’s working length precisely, for predictable root canals.

  • Rotary root canal motor

    Shapes canals smoothly with some of the best file systems available.

  • Ultrasonic irrigation

    Cleans canals more thoroughly, and helps find missed canals in re-treatments.

  • Digital X-ray

    An intraoral sensor shows X-rays instantly on screen, with far less radiation.

  • 3D CBCT planning

    A three-dimensional view of the jaw to plan implants around bone, nerves and anatomy.

  • Implant motor

    Real-time feedback on insertion torque and implant stability during placement.

  • PRF regeneration

    Platelet-rich fibrin from your own blood helps bone and gums heal after grafting and implants.

  • Electrosurgery

    Minor gum procedures with greater precision and less bleeding than a scalpel.

  • Intraoral camera

    See the inside of your own mouth on screen and decide with full information.

  • Low-noise drills

    Smooth, quiet, low-vibration handpieces for a calmer visit.

FAQ

Good questions.

Are your prices really fixed?

Yes. Our charges are published openly and valid till December 2026. Your exact plan, and any extra steps like a post or 3D scan, is explained before treatment starts.

Is root canal treatment painful?

The area is numbed first, so you should feel only a small prick. Once the tooth is numb, you should not feel pain. Every root canal is done under a microscope for precision.

How many visits does a root canal take?

It depends on the infection. Simple cases can be done in one sitting, but the aim is to remove the infection completely, not to rush.

What is software-guided implant surgery?

Your jaw is scanned in 3D, the implant is planned on software, and a custom guide is used to place it precisely, usually through a small opening instead of cutting the gums.

Which day is the clinic closed?

Tuesday is our weekly off. We are open on weekends, 9:30 am to 1:30 pm and 4:30 pm to 8:30 pm.

Where exactly is the clinic?

Ground floor, Park View Apartments, opposite Mother Dairy, Sector 29, Noida. Botanical Garden and Sector 18 metro stations are walking distance away.
