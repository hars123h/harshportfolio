FAQ

Quick answers.

Does whitening hurt?

Most people feel nothing. Some feel a mild tingle for a day, so we use a sensitivity-safe gel and check in between rounds.

How long do aligners take?

Six to twelve months for most smiles. Your 3D plan shows the exact timeline before you start.

Do you take walk-ins?

For emergencies, always. For everything else, book in 30 seconds above and skip the wait.

Is there parking?

Yes, free valet parking at the door.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. No-cost EMI up to 12 months on aligners and implants, and UPI or cards for everything.

Do you treat kids?

From their first tooth. Kids’ visits are short, fun and parents stay in the room.
