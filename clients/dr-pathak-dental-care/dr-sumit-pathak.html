About the clinic

The specialist behind the smile.

Dr. Pathak Dental Care was set up so that people in Greater Noida could see a prosthodontist close to home, at hours that suit working families: 9 am to 9 pm.

Your dentist

Dr. Sumit Pathak, prosthodontist.

A prosthodontist is a dentist with three more years of specialist training (MDS) in replacing and rebuilding teeth: implants, crowns, bridges and dentures. Dr. Pathak trained in Greater Noida, led a Clove Dental clinic for three years, and now runs his own practice in Alpha 2.

  1. 2010BDSKrishna Dental College
  2. 2015MDS, Prosthodontics and Crown & BridgeI.T.S Dental College, Greater Noida
  3. 2015–16ResidentHindu Rao Hospital, Delhi

Areas of focus

What Dr. Pathak does most

Dental implants

A fixed new tooth on a titanium root. Nothing to take out, nothing trimmed next door.

Dentures

Full, partial, BPS and implant-held dentures that grip, bite and look natural.

Crowns & bridges

Caps that protect weak teeth, and bridges that fill a gap. Zirconia, ceramic or metal-ceramic.

Smile design

Whitening, bonding, edge reshaping and veneers, planned around your face.

Braces & aligners

Tooth-coloured ceramic braces and clear aligners for teens and adults.

Root canal treatment

Stops the pain and saves your own tooth, usually in one or two visits.
