Children

Paediatric dentistry

Children won’t keep their first teeth forever, but those teeth matter: they help a child bite, chew and speak, and hold the space the permanent teeth will erupt into.

Book a visit0120 429 8846

Why milk teeth matter

Primary teeth guide the eruption of permanent teeth. As permanent crowns push against the roots of milk teeth, the roots resorb, letting each new tooth take its proper position. For the first six years or so, a child relies on milk teeth alone.

How we protect them

Topical fluoride

A higher concentration of fluoride than toothpaste, applied to harden the enamel against decay.

Dental sealants

Seal the pits and fissures on back teeth, where decay-causing bacteria thrive. Easy to apply, years of protection.

Root canal treatment

Saving a milk tooth from early loss helps prevent a bad bite that would need braces later.

Bonding

Chips and small fractures, common in childhood, repaired with life-like tooth-coloured material.
