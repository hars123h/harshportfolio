Questions

Before you come in.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, but booked patients are seen first. Book online or call, and we can usually fit you in the same day.

Is treatment covered by insurance?

We are cashless with 20+ insurers and corporate dental plans. For the rest, you get an itemised bill to claim from your insurer.

What if I have a dental emergency at night?

Call our emergency line. A doctor calls you back within 30 minutes and sees you the same night if it can’t wait until morning.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Treatments above ₹10,000 can be paid in 3, 6 or 9 monthly instalments at no extra cost.

When should my child first see a dentist?

By their first birthday, or within six months of the first tooth. Early visits are short and mostly about getting comfortable.
