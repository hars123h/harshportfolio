how a website project runs

How it runs

You get a working link by day 3, not a PDF of mockups. AI projects follow the same rhythm over 2 to 4 weeks, with a test set agreed on day 1.

  1. Day 1

    20-minute call

    You tell me how the business runs. I ask what a good month looks like.

  2. Day 2

    Scope and fixed price

    One page in plain words: what gets built, what doesn't, the price and the date.

  3. Day 3

    First live draft

    A real link you can open on your phone and show your team.

  4. Day 4 to 6

    Two rounds of changes

    You send notes on WhatsApp. I make them the same day.

  5. Day 7

    Launch and handover

    Domain, Google profile, analytics, and a 10-minute video on editing it yourself.

Who hosts it?

It runs on your own account (Vercel or Netlify). Most small sites cost ₹0 a month to host.

Who owns the code?

You do. The full code goes to your GitHub on launch day.

Can I edit it myself?

Yes. If you want, I add a simple panel for text, prices and photos. Bigger changes are a message away.

before you message

Questions

What founders and business owners usually ask before we start.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Two kinds. For startups: complete web products, dashboards and AI features, built end to end. For businesses: fast websites and landing pages that bring in enquiries. If your project is something else, ask. I’ll tell you honestly if I’m not the right fit.

How much will my project cost?

It depends on what needs to be built, so I don’t guess before we talk. After a 20-minute call you get a fixed price in writing, before any work starts. No hourly billing and no surprise invoices.

How long does it take?

A landing page or business website takes about 7 days. An AI feature usually takes 2 to 4 weeks, and a full product takes longer, depending on scope. You get the timeline in writing with the price, and a working link to check early on.

Can you build my whole product, from idea to launch?

Yes: frontend, backend, database, login, payments, AI and deployment. Little Legends was built that way, end to end. I also help you cut the first version down to what you need to launch and test with real users.

Can you add AI to my product or business?

Yes, where it saves real time or brings in money: assistants that answer from your own documents, automating email, invoice or lead work, and AI features inside your app. I work with OpenAI, Claude, Gemini and Bedrock. If AI won’t help, I’ll say so.

I already have a website. Can you fix or redesign it?

Yes. I start with a free check of speed, mobile layout and Google visibility, then tell you whether a fix or a rebuild makes more sense, and what each would cost.

Do I need to write the content?

No. I write the first draft from our call, your current site or your Google profile. You only correct it, which usually takes about 20 minutes.

How do payments work?

Usually 50% to start and 50% before launch. Bigger projects are split into milestones, so you pay as each part is delivered and approved.

What happens after launch?

You get 30 days of free bug fixes. After that, I can stay on for updates and new features, billed monthly or per change, whichever suits you.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I’ve worked with teams in Japan, Canada and the US. We stay in touch over WhatsApp, email or Slack, calls fit between 9am and 9pm IST, and payments work by bank transfer or Wise.
