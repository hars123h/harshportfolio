Before you come in.

Will it hurt?

Most treatments are done under local anaesthetic, given slowly with a numbing gel first. Sedation is available for anxious patients.

Do you accept insurance?

We give you an itemised bill for reimbursement and work with most corporate dental plans. Ask us about yours.

How long is an implant visit?

Placing the post takes about an hour. The crown goes on after the bone has healed, usually 8 to 12 weeks later.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Implants and aligners can be paid in no-cost monthly instalments through our partner.
