Treatments

Everything your smile needs, under one roof.

Specialist work in replacing and restoring teeth, plus the everyday care that keeps them healthy.

Dental implants

A fixed new tooth on a titanium root. Nothing to take out, nothing trimmed next door.

Dentures

Full, partial, BPS and implant-held dentures that grip, bite and look natural.

Crowns & bridges

Caps that protect weak teeth, and bridges that fill a gap. Zirconia, ceramic or metal-ceramic.

Smile design

Braces & aligners

Root canal treatment

Why a prosthodontist

Not just a tooth. Your whole bite.

General dentists do excellent work. When a tooth has to be replaced or rebuilt, a prosthodontist adds three years of training focused on exactly that.

  1. 01

    Your bite is planned

    Every crown, bridge or denture is designed around how your upper and lower teeth meet, so it lasts and feels right.

  2. 02

    Your face is part of the plan

    Teeth support the lips and cheeks. Replacing them well can soften a sunken look and bring back a natural smile.

  3. 03

    One plan, start to finish

    From the first scan to the final crown, the same specialist plans and carries out the work, so nothing is lost in between.
