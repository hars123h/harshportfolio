Treatment

Dentures, crowns & bridges

Dentures are artificial teeth that replace some or all missing teeth. They help you chew, speak clearly and smile with confidence, and keep the face and mouth in the right alignment.

Book a consultationAsk on WhatsApp
For
Missing, broken or heavily filled teeth
Options

Specialist
Dr. Varun Gaur, prosthodontist

Options

Complete dentures

Replace all the teeth in the upper or lower jaw.

Partial dentures

Fill the gaps when some natural teeth remain.

Implant-supported dentures

Clip onto two or more implants, so the denture stays firm while you eat and speak.

Crowns & bridges

Caps that restore damaged teeth, and fixed bridges that replace missing ones.

Full-mouth rehabilitation

A complete plan to restore function and appearance when many teeth need work.
