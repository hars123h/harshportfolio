Dental imaging: X-rays, CBCT & intraoral cameras

Good treatment starts with seeing clearly. Imaging shows what the eye can’t, from a hidden cavity to the exact shape of your jawbone.

Types of imaging

Dental X-rays

A two-dimensional view that reveals cavities, bone levels, impacted teeth and abscesses.

CBCT 3D scan

A detailed 3D image of teeth, jaw and facial structures, used to plan implants, assess jaw-joint (TMJ) problems and evaluate complex cases.

Intraoral camera

A small handheld camera that shows the inside of your mouth on screen, so you see what the dentist sees.

Why it matters

  • Accurate diagnosis and a plan made for you
  • Precise planning for implants, braces and surgery
  • Less invasive procedures and quicker recovery
  • You understand your options before deciding
