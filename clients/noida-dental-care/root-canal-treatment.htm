Treatment

Root canal treatment

Every root canal under a microscope, for precision and long-lasting results.

Call 96509 83999From ₹7,000 with crown

01 / 07

  1. 01

    The problem

    Decay or injury lets bacteria reach the pulp: the nerves and blood vessels inside the tooth. That causes sensitivity, pain, swelling or a darkening tooth.

  2. 02

    Numb, gently

    The gum is numbed with a spray first, then the tooth. Once it is numb, you should feel no pain.

  3. 03

    Isolate with a rubber dam

    A soft rubber sheet seals the tooth off from the rest of your mouth: a bacteria-free field, and no chemicals or debris at the back of your throat.

  4. 04

    See it at 16×

    Every root canal here is done under an operating microscope, so even the tiniest canal is found and treated, and the most tooth structure is saved.

  5. 05

    Measure and clean

    An apex locator measures each canal’s exact working length. Rotary files shape the canals and ultrasonic irrigation flushes out bacteria and debris.

  6. 06

    Seal in 3D

    The canals are sealed with warm material that flows into every branch, including accessory canals, leaving no space for bacteria to return.

  7. 07

    Protect with a crown

    A cap restores the tooth’s strength so it lasts for years. In some cases a simple composite filling is enough.

Signs not to ignore

  • Sensitivity to hot and cold
  • Pus, a boil on the gum, or bad breath
  • Swelling in the gums or face
  • A tooth that has darkened, even without pain
  • Pain that keeps coming back after painkillers. Please don’t keep self-medicating; it spreads to surrounding teeth.

Will it last?

Success depends on how well the canals are shaped, cleaned and sealed. With the microscope, the right equipment and the best materials, success rates are very high. Keep up your oral hygiene and visit every six months.

Questions

What causes the need for a root canal?

Mostly decay that is left untreated: food deposits lead to bacteria, enamel erosion and decay that eventually reaches the pulp. Injury can also cause it. Regular check-ups catch decay while a filling is still enough.

How is the diagnosis made?

Dr. Anjana records your past treatment, symptoms and any self-medication, then examines the tooth, takes an X-ray, and checks the nerve with a cold test and an electric pulp test.

Is it painless?

You will feel a small prick as the area is numbed; after that, you should not feel pain.

How many visits?

It depends on the infection. Simple cases can be finished in one sitting, but the aim is to remove the infection completely, not to rush.

Is there an alternative?

If the roots are infected, root canal treatment is the only way to save the tooth. Many severely decayed teeth that would normally be extracted have been saved here.

Do I need to prepare?

No. You can eat beforehand and take any medicines prescribed. The tooth is numbed before treatment starts.
