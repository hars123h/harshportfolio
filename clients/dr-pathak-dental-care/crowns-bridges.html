Treatments / Crowns & bridges

Caps and bridges that blend in.

A crown covers a weak, broken or root-treated tooth to protect it. A bridge uses crowns on the teeth either side of a gap to hold a false tooth in between. Dr. Pathak’s MDS is in Prosthodontics and Crown & Bridge, so this is the heart of his work.

Is it for you?

You might need this if…

  • A tooth is cracked, broken or heavily filled
  • You’ve had a root canal on a back tooth
  • One or two teeth are missing and the teeth beside the gap already need crowns
  • An old crown looks dark at the gum line

What to expect

How it goes, visit by visit

  1. 1

    Check and plan

    An X-ray, then we choose the material and shade together, in daylight.

  2. 2

    Prepare the tooth

    The tooth is numbed and shaped so the crown fits over it. A temporary crown goes on the same day.

  3. 3

    Impression or scan

    An accurate copy of your teeth goes to the dental lab, with your shade and photos.

  4. 4

    Fit

    The crown or bridge is checked for fit, bite and colour before it is cemented.

FAQ

Questions people ask

How long does a crown last?

Many crowns last 10–15 years or longer with good brushing and regular check-ups.

Bridge or implant?

If the teeth either side are healthy, an implant avoids trimming them. If they already need crowns, a bridge does two jobs at once. Try the smile planner on our home page, or ask at your visit.

Will a crown look fake?

It shouldn’t. The shade is matched to your own teeth, and for front teeth all-ceramic crowns let light through like natural enamel.

Does preparing the tooth hurt?

No, the tooth is numbed first. Some people feel mild sensitivity for a day or two afterwards.
