Good to know

Questions parents ask us.

My child is scared of the dentist. What do you do?

The first visit is a tour: they meet the chair, the light and the little mirror before anything else. We go at their pace, and many first visits end with just counting teeth.

Can the whole family come in one appointment?

Yes. Book once and we line up back-to-back slots, so up to four people are done in about two hours.

Do you see emergencies?

Every day, including Sunday mornings. Call us and we’ll fit you in the same day.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking in the building basement, and the clinic is on the ground floor.

See you this week?

Book one visit or the whole family. We confirm on WhatsApp within the hour.
