FAQ

Good questions.

Do aligners hurt?

You’ll feel pressure for a day or two with each new tray. That’s the teeth moving. Most people describe it as tight, not painful.

Will people notice them?

Rarely. The trays are clear and thin. Up close, someone might notice a slight shine.

Can I talk normally?

A slight lisp for the first two or three days, then your tongue adjusts.

What if I lose a tray?

Move to the next tray and tell us on WhatsApp. Replacements are included on Complete and Teen plans.

Is the 3D scan really free?

Yes. You get the scan, a preview of your final smile and a written plan, with no obligation.

Do I need a retainer after?

Yes, at night. Teeth like to drift back. Your first retainer is included in every plan.
