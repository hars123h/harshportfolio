Full mouth rehabilitation

When many teeth are worn, broken or missing, full mouth rehabilitation rebuilds your bite, chewing and smile together as one planned treatment.

Book a visit0120 429 8846

Who it’s for

  • Many missing or broken teeth
  • Teeth badly worn down by grinding or acid
  • A bite that has collapsed over time
  • Several failing old restorations

How it’s planned

  1. DiagnosisTeeth, gums, bite and jaw joints are examined, with X-rays and models.
  2. PlanCrowns, bridges, implants, root canals and dentures are combined into one plan.
  3. Treatment in stagesDone step by step, so you are never without teeth.
  4. MaintenanceRegular reviews keep the result healthy.
