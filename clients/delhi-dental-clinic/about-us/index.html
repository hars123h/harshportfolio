Our doctors

Meet your dentists.

Implantologist & oral surgeon

Dr. Nirmaljeet Singh Arora

Honorary Senior Consultant, Batra Hospital

Dr. Arora has practised dentistry for over three decades and is an Honorary Senior Consultant at Batra Hospital. He completed his Bachelor of Dental Surgery with a compulsory internship and house job in General Dentistry at PGI Rohtak, after school at St. Joseph’s Academy, Dehradun.

He trained in implant prosthesis and aesthetics through the Nobel Fellowship Advance Programme with Nobel Biocare, Sweden, and is a registered Master Fellow and Diplomate of the World Congress of Oral Implantology (WCOI). A specialised residency in Maxillofacial Surgery at the Department of Burns, Plastic and Maxillofacial Surgery, Safdarjung Hospital, gave him deep experience in trauma and advanced oral surgery.

Beyond surgery he works across prosthodontics, endodontics and periodontics, with a special interest in how diabetes affects dental health. He is a certified product trainer for the YpsoPump System (Switzerland) for diabetic control, has served on a UPSC selection panel, and provides pro bono dental care with NGOs.

Aesthetic dentistry & endodontics

Dr. Nidhi Kohli Suri

Senior Consultant, Max Hospital

Dr. Nidhi Kohli Suri has been a dental professional since 2005, working in aesthetic dentistry and endodontics for patients of every age. She is a Senior Consultant at Max Hospital and at The Delhi Dental Clinic.

From 2007 to 2019 she was Scientific Affairs and Education Manager at 3M Oral Care Solutions. As a Level 1 trainer in aesthetic dentistry she lectured across India and Sri Lanka, advised clinics on management and Six Sigma, and promoted oral health through 3M’s Clinpro and “Health Care Academy” initiatives.

She places special emphasis on preventive dentistry for children, holds a Fellowship of the International College of Dentists, and was awarded a Gold Medal by the Cancer Aid Society.

  • Scientific Affairs & Education Manager, 3M Oral Care (2007–2019)
