FAQ

Good to know.

When is the clinic open?

Monday to Saturday from 10:30 am to 1:30 pm and 5:00 pm to 8:30 pm. Sunday from 10:30 am to 2:00 pm, and Sunday evening by appointment. The clinic is closed on Thursday.

Is the clinic empanelled with CGHS?

Yes, Dr. Gaur’s Dental Clinic is empanelled with CGHS. If you are a CGHS beneficiary, mention it when you book and the team will guide you through the process.

What does NABH accreditation mean?

NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers) accreditation means the clinic meets national standards for patient safety and quality of care, including sterilisation and infection control.

Is root canal treatment painful?

Root canals here are done by an endodontist under local anaesthesia, so the treatment itself is comfortable. Many patients describe it as painless.

Will I know the cost before treatment starts?

Yes. The doctor explains your treatment plan, the process and the estimated cost before anything begins.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Dr. Radhika is a paediatric dentist who looks after infants, children and teenagers. We recommend a first visit by your child’s first birthday.
