    Questions

    Before you decide.

    Will veneers look fake?

    Not if they’re designed for your face. We match shape and shade to your skin and age, and you wear a trial smile before anything is final.

    Do you have to file my teeth down?

    Usually only 0.3 to 0.5 mm, about the thickness of a fingernail. Some cases need no filing at all.

    How long do veneers last?

    Fifteen years or more with normal care. Our porcelain carries a 10-year guarantee.

    Can I pay in instalments?

    Yes, no-cost EMI for up to 12 months on any plan above ₹30,000.
