A neighbourhood clinic that grew up.

Fifteen years, five specialists and one rule: explain first, and write the price down before we start.

Muskaan opened in 2010 as a two-chair clinic above a pharmacy in Sector 29. Dr. Vikram Sethi wanted a practice where nobody was rushed and no bill came as a surprise.

Today we have five specialists, six treatment rooms, our own digital X-ray and 3D scanner, and a separate room for children.

What hasn’t changed is how we work. Every plan is explained on screen, every estimate is in writing, and the same doctor sees you from the first visit to the last.

Safety

How we keep every visit clean.

Sterilisation is the part of dentistry you never see. Here is exactly what happens to every instrument before it reaches you.

01

Cleaned

Instruments are scrubbed and run through an ultrasonic bath to remove anything left on them.

02

Sealed

Each set is packed into its own sealed pouch with a colour indicator.

03

Sterilised

Pouches go through a Class-B autoclave at 134 °C, the hospital standard.

04

Logged

Every cycle is recorded with a test strip, so each pouch can be traced.

05

Opened in front of you

Your pouch is opened only once you are in the chair, so you can see it.
