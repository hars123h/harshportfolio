01

Three parts. One tooth.

A titanium post replaces the root, a small abutment connects it, and a zirconia crown is the part people see. Each one is made for your mouth from a 3D scan.

02

Placed in the bone.

A 3D-printed guide sets the post at the exact depth and angle we planned. It takes about an hour under local anaesthetic, and most people are back at work the next day.

03

The bone grows onto it.

Over 8 to 12 weeks your jawbone bonds to the titanium. That bond is why implants last for decades. You wear a temporary tooth meanwhile, so nobody sees a gap.

04

A tooth you forget about.

The abutment and crown go on in one short visit. It looks like your own tooth, and you can bite into an apple again.

Questions

Asked on every first visit.

Still unsure? Bring your questions to the free consultation. We’ll go through all of them.

Is getting an implant painful?

It’s done under local anaesthetic, and most patients say it was easier than having a tooth pulled. Expect mild soreness for two or three days.

How long does an implant last?

With normal brushing and a yearly check-up, decades. The implant itself carries a 25-year warranty.

Am I too old for implants?

Age is rarely the problem. Bone and general health matter more, and we check both on your 3D scan. We regularly treat patients in their seventies and eighties.

What if I don’t have enough bone?

A bone graft or sinus lift builds it up first. You will know on your first visit, with the cost in writing.

How soon do I get my new tooth?

You get a temporary tooth the same week. The final crown goes on after 8 to 14 weeks. With All-on-4, fixed teeth are fitted within 72 hours.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. No-cost EMI for up to 12 months, and cashless treatment with major insurers.
