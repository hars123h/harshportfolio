What are dental implants?

Artificial tooth roots made of biocompatible titanium, placed in the jawbone. They give a strong foundation for replacement teeth and look and work like natural teeth.

Is getting an implant painful?

The area is numbed with local anaesthesia, so you should feel very little during the procedure. Mild soreness afterwards is common and is usually managed with prescribed or over-the-counter pain relief.

Can a root canal be done in one sitting?

Often, yes. Depending on the tooth and the infection, many root canals are completed in a single sitting.

When should my child first visit the dentist?

By their first birthday, or when the first tooth appears. Early, easy visits help children grow up comfortable at the dentist.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Usually between 6 and 30 months, sometimes longer. It depends on the patient’s age, how severe the problem is, how much movement is needed and how well the plan is followed.
