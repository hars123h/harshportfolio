FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the first appointment free?

You can book a free appointment through this website, on WhatsApp or by phone. The dentist examines your teeth and explains your options before any treatment starts.

How often should I visit the dentist for a check-up?

Usually every six months for a check-up and cleaning, though it can vary with your oral health. We will give you a personal recommendation at your visit.

What are the signs that I should see a dentist?

Tooth pain, swollen or bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, loose teeth and sensitivity to hot or cold are all reasons to book a visit.

Do you offer emergency dental care?

Yes. For a severe toothache, a knocked-out or broken tooth or any other dental emergency, call your nearest clinic straight away.

Can anxious children be treated comfortably?

Yes. Our paediatric dentist, Dr. Prasurjya Bora, is certified in nitrous oxide inhalation sedation, which helps nervous children relax during treatment.

How can I prevent cavities and gum disease?

Brush and floss every day, cut down on sugary snacks and drinks, avoid tobacco, and keep up regular check-ups and cleanings.
