Teeth whitening

In-clinic whitening, usually five to ten shades lighter in about an hour.

What to expect

Teeth usually become five to ten shades whiter, depending on their natural colour and the type of stain. Results last a couple of years, depending on tea, coffee and hygiene.

How it’s done

  1. CheckTeeth, existing dental work and enamel are assessed, and your goals discussed.
  2. CleanA routine scaling if needed, for an even result.
  3. ProtectA lip retractor and protective gel shield your gums.
  4. WhitenA professional-grade gel, boosted by a special light, applied a couple of times. About an hour in total.
