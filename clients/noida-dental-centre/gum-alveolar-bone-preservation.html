Gum & bone preservation

Bacteria and food particles constantly form a sticky film called plaque. If it is not removed, it hardens into tartar, which only a professional cleaning can remove.

Gingivitis and periodontitis

Plaque and tartar inflame the gums (gingivitis): red, swollen gums that bleed easily. Gingivitis can usually be reversed with daily brushing, flossing and regular professional cleaning.

Left untreated, it advances to periodontitis: gums pull away from the teeth and form infected pockets. Bacterial toxins and the body’s own response break down the bone and tissue that hold teeth in place, until teeth loosen and may have to be removed.

How it’s treated

A deep cleaning called scaling and root planing. Scaling removes tartar from above and below the gum line; root planing smooths rough spots on the root where germs gather.

Prevention is better than cure

We provide counselling and educational sessions on daily brushing and flossing, alongside regular professional cleanings and examinations.

The stages, step by step

Healthy

Pink, firm gums that fit snugly around the tooth. Bone level is normal.

Keep it up: brush, floss and get regular cleanings.

Gingivitis

Plaque and tartar inflame the gums: red, swollen, bleeding easily. No bone loss yet.

Reversible with daily brushing, flossing and a professional cleaning.

Periodontitis

Gums pull away and form infected pockets. Bacterial toxins start breaking down the bone.

Needs scaling and root planing: deep cleaning above and below the gum line.

Advanced

Bone and tissue are destroyed. Teeth loosen and may have to be removed.

Treat early: don’t wait for teeth to feel loose.
