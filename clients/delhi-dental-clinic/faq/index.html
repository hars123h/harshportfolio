General

What services does The Delhi Dental Clinic offer?

A complete range: routine check-ups, cleaning, fillings, root canal treatment, crowns and bridges, dental implants, braces and aligners, cosmetic dentistry, children’s dentistry, oral surgery and more, so the whole family can be looked after in one place.

How do I book an appointment?

Call or WhatsApp us on 098101 06117, or send the booking form on this website. We’ll get back to you to fix a time that suits you.

Can a root canal be done in one sitting?

Often, yes. Depending on the tooth and the infection, many root canals are completed in a single sitting.

How long does a crown last?

With good oral hygiene, a well-made crown lasts for many years.

Implants

What are dental implants?

Artificial tooth roots made of biocompatible titanium, placed in the jawbone. They give a strong foundation for replacement teeth and look and work like natural teeth.

How long do dental implants last?

With good care (daily brushing and flossing, and regular check-ups), implants can last a lifetime.

Is getting an implant painful?

The area is numbed with local anaesthesia, so you should feel very little during the procedure. Mild soreness afterwards is common and is usually managed with prescribed or over-the-counter pain relief.

Are there any risks with implants?

Implants have a high success rate, but like any surgery there are possible risks, such as infection, implant failure or damage to nearby structures. Careful planning and established protocols keep these risks low.

Can implants replace several missing teeth?

Yes. Implants can replace a single tooth, several teeth or a full arch. The dentist will assess your case and recommend the most suitable plan.

Braces

At what age should my child see an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first orthodontic check by age 7. Early detection helps avoid more difficult problems later.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Usually between 6 and 30 months, sometimes longer. It depends on the patient’s age, how severe the problem is, how much movement is needed and how well the plan is followed.

Children

When should my child first visit the dentist?

By their first birthday, or when the first tooth appears. Early, easy visits help children grow up comfortable at the dentist.

Cosmetic

Do you offer cosmetic dentistry?

Yes. We offer teeth whitening, veneers, dental bonding, reshaping and complete smile makeovers to improve how your teeth look and how you feel about your smile.

How long does laser whitening take?

About 45 minutes, in a single visit. A professional home kit is also available if you prefer to whiten gradually over 3 to 4 weeks.

From abroad

I live abroad. Can I plan treatment before I travel?

Yes. Send your X-rays or clear photos and a short note on WhatsApp or email. We’ll suggest a plan and how many days to set aside before you book your trip.

Still have a question?

Ask us directly. A real person at the clinic will answer.
