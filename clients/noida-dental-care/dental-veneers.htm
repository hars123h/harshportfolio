Composite or porcelain?

Bioclear composite veneers

Tooth-coloured composite shaped directly on your tooth. Usually one sitting, and more affordable.

Porcelain veneers

Thin porcelain shells custom-made in a lab. At least two sittings.

The Bioclear advantage

Instead of building a veneer in many layers, the Bioclear method flows heated composite around the tooth inside a clear matrix, creating a single monolithic restoration that is stronger and looks more natural.

One sitting, six steps

  1. DiscloseA solution reveals the biofilm on the tooth.
  2. BlastFine powder gently cleans it away.
  3. MatrixA matrix as thin as a hair is placed along the tooth.
  4. EtchOpens the tooth’s pores for a strong bond.
  5. InjectHeated composite fills the matrix as one piece.
  6. PolishShaped and polished to the final form.
