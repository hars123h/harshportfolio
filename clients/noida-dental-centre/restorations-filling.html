Restorations & fillings

A filling restores a decayed or damaged tooth to its normal shape, appearance and function, and stops the decay from spreading further.

Book a visit0120 429 8846

When you need a filling

  • A cavity found at a check-up or on an X-ray
  • Sensitivity to sweet, hot or cold food
  • A chipped or worn tooth
  • An old filling that is broken or leaking

How it’s done

  1. NumbThe tooth is numbed if needed.
  2. CleanDecay is removed and the tooth prepared.
  3. FillTooth-coloured material is placed in layers and hardened with light.
  4. ShapeThe filling is shaped to your bite and polished.
